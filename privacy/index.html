Privacy Policy

DroneSN Integration · Last updated: 21 November 2026

1. Scope

This policy covers DroneSN Integration, an internal tool used only by Drone Sarana Nusantara ("DroneSN", "we") to publish DroneSN's own videos to DroneSN's own accounts on TikTok, Instagram, Facebook and YouTube.

2. Data we access

When DroneSN connects its own account through a platform's official login, the tool receives only what is needed to publish:

  • Basic account information, such as display name and account or channel ID.
  • Access tokens and refresh tokens that allow uploading videos and checking upload status.

The tool does not collect data from other users, followers or viewers.

3. How we use it

Only to upload and publish videos created by DroneSN to DroneSN's own accounts, at the date and time DroneSN chooses, and to confirm whether each upload succeeded.

4. Storage and sharing

Tokens are stored locally on DroneSN's own computer. They are never sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising or profiling.

5. Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept only while DroneSN uses the tool and are deleted when it is no longer used. Access can be revoked at any time from each platform's settings, for example TikTok: Settings and privacy › Security › Manage app permissions; Google: myaccount.google.com/permissions; Meta: Business Integrations settings.
